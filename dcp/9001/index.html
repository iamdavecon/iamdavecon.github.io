Dave Quantity Measurement

1. Abstract

This document defines procedures for determining whether the current quantity of Dave exceeds acceptable measurement limits.

2. Measurement Inputs

Inputs MAY include visible Daves, DaveTracker presence, sticker density, badge count, simultaneous greetings, and the number of people who have begun saying “Dave” without context.

3. Thresholds

DAVE_COUNT = OBSERVED_DAVES + PROBABLE_DAVES + DAVE_ADJACENCY_FACTOR
OVERFLOW when DAVE_COUNT exceeds the local estimate of manageable Dave.

The local estimate is not standardized. This is the principal source of dispute.

4. Overflow Handling

On overflow, participants SHOULD distribute snacks, widen the hallway, and avoid attempting to identify the one true Dave. DCP-0002 MAY be suspended in favor of orderly movement.

5. Security Considerations

Counting people without consent is discouraged. Measurements SHOULD be approximate, temporary, and less important than the people being measured.
