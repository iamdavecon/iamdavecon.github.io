Durch/DaveCon Separation of Powers

1. Abstract

This document attempts to define jurisdictional boundaries between DaveCon and Durch. Publication of the standard resolved absolutely nothing.

2. Areas of Competence

DaveCon is presumed competent to organize gatherings, badges, stickers, field operations, and documentation. Durch is presumed competent to organize creeds, rites, states, and questions regarding the existence of Dave. These presumptions are non-binding.

3. Shared Jurisdiction

Dave identity, Dave ascension, Daveputization, hot dog pilgrimage, and any matter involving more than one official-looking seal are shared jurisdictions. Neither body is REQUIRED to acknowledge the other’s ruling.

4. Dispute Procedure

  1. Refer the question to the relevant authority.
  2. Receive an answer that changes the scope of the question.
  3. Publish a clarification.
  4. Refer the clarification back to the original authority.
  5. Declare the matter historically complicated.

Jurisdictional questions SHOULD be referred to this DCP, although this has historically made matters worse.

5. Supremacy

No supremacy clause is defined. A supremacy clause was proposed, debated, misplaced, and later cited as evidence that it had passed.

6. Security Considerations

Institutional ambiguity can cause duplicate ceremonies, conflicting badges, and two simultaneous explanations of the same event. Participants SHOULD follow venue rules and ordinary human decency when the authorities disagree.
