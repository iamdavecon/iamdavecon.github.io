Answer to Frequently Asked Dave Questions

1. Abstract

This document defines the canonical location for answers to frequently asked Dave questions. To prevent synchronization drift, the answers are maintained in the existing DaveCon FAQ.

2. Normative Answers

Questions regarding Dave identity, attendance, location, timing, cost, cancellation, family compatibility, and general conduct SHALL be referred to the FAQ. Implementations MUST NOT duplicate answers without a change-control review.

3. Caching

Cached answers MAY be retained for convenience. When a cached answer conflicts with the current FAQ, the current FAQ wins unless the cached answer is funnier.

4. Security Considerations

FAQ answers do not grant authority, credentials, or permission to approach, touch, deputize, or feed another Dave.
