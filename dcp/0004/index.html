Dave Ascension Procedure

1. Abstract

This document records recognized methods by which a Dave or Dave-adjacent entity may ascend to a higher Dave state. Durch authorities and DaveTracker implementations may disagree about both the starting state and the direction of travel.

2. Preconditions

The subject MUST satisfy DCP-0001 or be accepted by an authority that claims to satisfy it. Possession of a badge, sticker, hot dog, or compelling posture MAY increase ascension confidence but is not sufficient.

3. Recognized Methods

  1. Administrative ascension: a sufficiently authoritative Dave updates the subject’s classification.
  2. Ritual ascension: Durch recognizes a state change after an approved or improvised rite.
  3. Operational ascension: the subject performs an act of Daveputy-grade usefulness.
  4. Proximity ascension: the subject remains near Dave activity until classification becomes inevitable.

4. State Transition

UNVERIFIED → DAVE-ADJACENT → DAVE → ASCENDED
       ↘              ↗             ↘
             IMPLEMENTATION-SPECIFIC

A transition MAY be reversible, ceremonial, or merely reported. A chair is not REQUIRED, but has historically improved the visibility of the event.

5. Authority

Durch authorities MAY issue declarations. DaveTracker MAY display a conflicting state. In the event of disagreement, both states SHOULD remain visible until someone loses interest.

6. Security Considerations

Ascension MUST NOT be used to compel participation, override consent, or claim real-world authority. Subjects MAY decline ascension and remain operationally valid.
