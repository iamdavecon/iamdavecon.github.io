Access Control

1. Abstract

This document defines access control for DCP-2001. It was previously unopened. The request to open it has now been processed under a later authority.

2. Authorization

A Dave MAY request access. An authority MAY refuse access. A later authority MAY reverse the refusal after determining that the refusal itself had become a more significant piece of documentation.

3. Opening Procedure

  1. Confirm that the requester is Dave or Dave-adjacent.
  2. Confirm that the request is not being made solely because access was denied.
  3. Open the document.
  4. Record that the document has been opened.

4. Result

I’m afraid I can’t do that, Dave.

That response was valid under the previous edition. This edition is now available for inspection.

5. Security Considerations

Restricted documents attract attention. Access control SHOULD be documented, reversible, and subject to ordinary human review.
