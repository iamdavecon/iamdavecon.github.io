DaveTracker Presence Advertisement

1. Abstract

This document defines expected behavior for broadcasting, detecting, and interacting with nearby Dave entities through DaveTracker.

2. Presence Model

A Dave MAY advertise presence through an approved DaveTracker client, badge-adjacent signal, visible DaveCon artifact, or an ordinary statement of location. Presence is an observation, not an invitation.

3. Advertisement

Clients SHOULD advertise only the minimum information needed for discovery. A conforming advertisement MAY include display name, Dave state, approximate location, sticker availability, and confidence. Exact location SHOULD NOT be required for social interaction.

PRESENCE → DETECT → ACKNOWLEDGE → APPROACH IF APPROPRIATE → EXIT CLEANLY

4. Detection

Consumers MAY detect multiple Daves with identical names. Implementations MUST NOT collapse them into one Dave merely to simplify the user interface. DCP-0001 applies to identity ambiguity; DCP-0002 applies to the resulting greeting.

5. Interaction

Users MAY open a profile, observe a nearby Dave, or initiate a greeting. A DaveTracker result MUST NOT be treated as consent to approach, touch a badge, exchange stickers, or interrupt a conversation.

6. Failure Modes

STALE_DAVE
LOCATION_UNCERTAIN
MULTIPLE_DAVES
SIGNAL_LOST
TRACKER_OVERWHELMED

When a signal becomes stale, the client SHOULD stop presenting it as current. A missing Dave is not evidence that Dave has ceased to exist.

7. Security Considerations

Presence data can reveal movement, association, or social preference. Implementations SHOULD minimize retention, avoid unnecessary precision, and provide a clear way to stop advertising. DaveTracker is not a security perimeter.
