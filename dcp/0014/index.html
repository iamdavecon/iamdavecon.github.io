DaveCon Badge Battery Preservation

1. Abstract

This document defines procedures for conserving badge power while maintaining sufficient illumination for admiration and emergency identification.

2. Power States

FULL → ADMIRABLE → CONSERVATION → BLINKING UNCERTAINLY → DARK

3. Requirements

Badges SHOULD use sleep states when unattended. Operators MUST NOT repeatedly press undocumented buttons solely to confirm that the badge remains alive. Observers MAY admire a dark badge based on prior evidence.

4. Emergency Identification

A badge MAY remain active during low-light navigation, lost-Dave recovery, or a situation in which its owner is trying to be found by someone holding a hot dog.

5. Security Considerations

Battery preservation MUST NOT disable safety lights, accessibility features, or consent indicators. Charging equipment SHOULD be supervised in crowded areas.
