Definition of Dave

1. Abstract

This document attempts to establish the minimum technical requirements necessary for an entity to qualify as a Dave. The resulting definition is intentionally broad, operationally convenient, and not especially useful.

Implementation compatibility remains poor. Two conforming Daves MAY disagree about whether either party is conforming.

2. Terminology

Dave: An entity satisfying at least one normative Dave criterion in Section 3, or an entity accepted as Dave by a sufficiently authoritative Dave.

Dave-adjacent: An entity demonstrating compatible behavior without satisfying the minimum identity requirements.

Authoritative Dave: A Dave who has been treated as authoritative by at least one other Dave. This definition is recursive by design.

3. Normative Requirements

  1. A Dave MAY self-identify as Dave.
  2. An entity MAY be declared Dave by another sufficiently authoritative Dave.
  3. Possession of DaveCon artifacts is neither necessary nor sufficient for Dave status.
  4. Participation in DaveCon increases confidence but does not establish Dave status.
  5. A Dave MUST NOT be rejected merely because another Dave has the same name.
  6. DavePrime represents a special case and SHOULD NOT be used as the reference implementation.

4. Interoperability Considerations

Systems implementing this standard SHOULD account for Daves named David, non-Daves named Dave, honorary Daves, Dave-adjacent entities, and implementation-specific behavior. A David MAY be an expanded Dave or an unrelated identifier. This document does not resolve the matter.

Implementations MUST permit multiple Daves to coexist without name collision. Systems that fail this requirement are likely to be legacy systems and MAY be handled according to DCP-1337, which is deprecated and therefore difficult to locate.

5. Conformance

A participant claiming conformance SHOULD be able to answer “Who is Dave?” without immediately forwarding the question to another participant. This requirement is advisory because the canonical answer remains context-dependent.

Result: the standard produces a definition of Dave that is sufficiently interoperable for conversation and insufficiently precise for software.

6. Security Considerations

False Dave claims may result in sticker imbalance, unauthorized badge inspection, or accidental elevation to Daveputy. Implementations MUST respect ordinary human consent regardless of status.

7. Durch Considerations

Durch MAY recognize additional Dave states. Such states are outside the scope of this document. Ascension is defined, to the extent it is defined, in DCP-0004; the document is currently disputed and has not been published in full.
