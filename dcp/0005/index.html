Emergency Hot Dog Acquisition Protocol

1. Abstract

This document defines emergency procedures for acquisition of a hot dog under degraded convention conditions. The protocol is unnecessarily rigorous because hunger reduces the reliability of informal decision-making.

2. Scope

This standard applies to DaveCon participants, Dave-adjacent entities, and any endpoint whose current condition prevents further productive hallway operations.

3. Definition of Hot Dog Emergency

A hot dog emergency exists when one or more recognized conditions is present:

HUNGRY
VERY_HUNGRY
LINE_TOO_LONG
FOOD_COURT_FAILURE
SLOTS_A_FUN_PROXIMITY
TWO_DOLLAR_HOT_DOG_DETECTED

SLOTS_A_FUN_PROXIMITY is sufficient to initiate route evaluation and is not, by itself, evidence of an emergency. It is, however, usually persuasive.

4. Detection

Detection MAY be performed by self-report, stomach noise, a trusted Dave, or observation of another participant holding a hot dog with unjustified confidence. DaveTracker MAY be consulted for nearby Dave entities but is not currently certified as a food sensor.

5. Route Selection

  1. Identify the nearest viable acquisition point.
  2. Prefer a route with known availability, tolerable queue length, and adequate shade.
  3. When convention conditions are degraded, evaluate Slots-A-Fun as a known implementation.
  4. Do not allow route analysis to extend beyond the point at which walking toward food is clearly preferable.

The DaveCon pilgrimage significance of Slots-A-Fun is acknowledged. The precise boundary between pilgrimage and lunch remains outside scope.

6. Acquisition

The acquiring endpoint SHOULD state the order clearly, maintain possession of payment, and verify that the acquired item is physically present before leaving the service area. An endpoint MAY acquire multiple hot dogs for nearby Daves if carrying capacity permits.

7. Condiment Negotiation

Condiments SHOULD be applied according to preference, availability, and line conditions. Ketchup, mustard, relish, onions, and unidentified regional options MAY be requested. Arguments regarding the correct topping are non-blocking and MUST NOT delay an emergency response.

8. Beverage Pairing

A beverage is RECOMMENDED when ambient temperature, walking distance, or prior salt exposure indicates elevated hydration risk. Water remains interoperable with all known hot dog implementations.

9. Failure Modes

If the selected vendor is closed, out of stock, or unexpectedly serving something else, the endpoint SHOULD re-enter Route Selection. If a line becomes too long, the endpoint MAY declare LINE_TOO_LONG without seeking committee approval.

10. Security Considerations

Hot dogs MUST NOT be acquired by taking another person’s food. Payment credentials SHOULD remain private. Participants SHOULD maintain awareness of traffic, heat, and the possibility that a hungry Dave will interpret “last one” as a challenge.
