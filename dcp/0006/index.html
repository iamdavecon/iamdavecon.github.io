Badge-to-Badge Interaction Guidelines

1. Abstract

This document covers safe inspection, admiration, blinking-light observation, SAO attachment, and other interactions between badges and their interested endpoints.

2. Scope

This standard applies to wearable badges, conference badges, SAOs, badge-adjacent electronics, and observers who have entered the badge radius.

3. Inspection

  1. An observer MAY admire a badge from a respectful distance.
  2. An observer SHOULD ask before picking up, turning over, or closely inspecting another person’s badge.
  3. The badge owner MAY decline inspection without supplying a technical explanation.
  4. Observation of blinking lights MUST NOT be treated as permission to press buttons.

4. SAO Attachment

SAO attachment MAY occur when both endpoints agree on orientation, mounting method, and acceptable risk. An attachment MUST NOT obstruct a wearer’s movement, vision, accessibility equipment, or existing badge controls.

5. Badge-to-Badge Negotiation

Two badge owners MAY compare features, exchange firmware lore, or establish a temporary demonstration link. Cable, pin, magnetic, wireless, and unexplained glowing interfaces are all implementation-specific.

6. Error Conditions

OWNER_NOT_PRESENT
DO_NOT_TOUCH
BATTERY_LOW
MOUNTING_UNCERTAIN
BUTTON_UNDOCUMENTED

When DO_NOT_TOUCH is observed or communicated, the observer MUST withdraw. A badge that appears to invite interaction may still be attached to a person who does not.

7. Security Considerations

Badges may contain batteries, sharp edges, exposed contacts, personal data, or a button that starts a presentation. Physical consent and electrical caution take precedence over curiosity.
