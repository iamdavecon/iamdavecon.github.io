Daveputization Protocol

1. Abstract

This document defines the process by which an otherwise ordinary participant may be deputized into a Daveputy. Authority delegation is intentionally unclear.

2. Appointment

A Daveputy MAY be appointed by a Dave, a Dope, a Durch representative, a badge-bearing witness, or a group that has already begun acting as though the appointment occurred.

3. Powers

A Daveputy MAY point at a problem, explain a convention, locate a hot dog, or remind nearby Daves to ask before touching a badge. No legal, venue, security, or financial powers are conveyed.

4. Insignia

Visible DaveCon artifacts MAY support recognition but MUST NOT be treated as proof of authority. A marker, sticker, lanyard, or confident posture is not a credential.

5. Revocation

Appointment MAY be revoked by the appointing authority, the appointee, or the practical discovery that nobody knows who appointed anyone.

6. Security Considerations

Daveputization MUST NOT be used to compel access, movement, contact, or personal information. Ordinary consent and venue rules remain authoritative.
