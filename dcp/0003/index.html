Sticker Exchange Standard

1. Abstract

This document defines a best-effort protocol for transferring adhesive-backed Dave artifacts between consenting endpoints.

2. Exchange Model

An endpoint MAY offer, accept, decline, or defer a sticker. Exchange SHOULD NOT require equivalent consideration. A sticker may be given freely without creating a debt, alliance, or obligation to locate a comparable sticker.

3. Negotiation

  1. Establish that the receiving endpoint is interested.
  2. Present the sticker without applying it to property not controlled by the recipient.
  3. Confirm placement, surface, and reversibility where relevant.
  4. Complete the exchange and return to ordinary Dave operations.

4. Rejection

A declined sticker MUST be withdrawn without persuasion. “No thank you” is a successful termination signal.

5. Security Considerations

Adhesive can damage surfaces, obscure labels, or create social pressure. Consent and venue rules take precedence over protocol completion.
