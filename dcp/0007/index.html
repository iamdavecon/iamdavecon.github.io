Googly Eye Deployment Standard

1. Abstract

This document defines acceptable placement, density, target suitability, and reversibility requirements for googly-eye deployment. It is proposed because the previous informal process produced several eyes on a coffee machine and no minutes.

2. Deployment Classes

Targets SHOULD be classified as stationary decorative, temporary exhibit, mobile consenting, or critical infrastructure. The fourth class exists primarily to prohibit itself.

3. Normative Requirements

  1. Deployment MUST be reversible without material damage.
  2. Density SHOULD be proportional to target area and the target’s apparent ability to tolerate whimsy.
  3. Eyes SHOULD be placed so that ordinary operation remains possible.
  4. Deployment against non-consenting critical infrastructure is NOT RECOMMENDED.
  5. Targets belonging to another person MUST be treated as non-consenting until permission is obtained.

4. Target Suitability

Suitable targets MAY include personal props, temporary signage, disposable packaging, consenting badges, and objects that have clearly entered a googly-eye program. Unsuitable targets include emergency equipment, screens displaying instructions, vehicles in motion, and anything currently preventing someone from finding a hot dog.

5. Removal

The deploying endpoint is responsible for removal at the end of the operational period. Adhesive residue, missing eyes, and unexplained facial expressions MUST be remediated where practical.

6. Security Considerations

Googly eyes can alter perceived threat posture, create confusion in low light, and cause observers to anthropomorphize infrastructure. Deployment MUST yield to safety, accessibility, venue rules, and consent.
