Dave-to-Dave Greeting Protocol

1. Abstract

This document defines procedures for initial Dave discovery, acknowledgment, optional :D exchange, sticker negotiation, optional badge inspection, and graceful termination of the encounter. It treats a human interaction as a network handshake for administrative clarity.

2. Terminology

Endpoint: A person or Dave-adjacent entity participating in the exchange. ACK: Any unambiguous acknowledgment, including “hello.” Optional interaction: An interaction requiring normal human consent.

3. Protocol Overview

DISCOVERY → ACK → :D → STICKER_NEGOTIATION → OPTIONAL_BADGE_INSPECTION → COMPLETE

Participants MAY omit any optional phase. A participant MAY simply say hello and continue walking. This is a fully conforming implementation.

4. Discovery

Discovery MAY occur through name recognition, badge observation, DaveTracker presence, sticker proximity, introduction by a third party, or a compelling sense that a Dave has entered the area. A participant SHOULD avoid staring while attempting discovery.

5. Handshake

  1. The initiating endpoint SHOULD establish visual or verbal contact.
  2. The receiving endpoint MAY return an ACK by saying hello, waving, nodding, or presenting an equivalent signal.
  3. Either endpoint MAY transmit :D. The other endpoint is not REQUIRED to mirror it.
  4. Failure to receive an ACK MUST NOT be interpreted as hostility.

6. Sticker Negotiation

Sticker exchange SHOULD follow successful acknowledgment but MAY occur independently when venue conditions support it. Sticker exchange is defined in DCP-0003. Equivalent consideration is not REQUIRED; a sticker may be offered without expectation of repayment.

Participants MUST ask before attaching a sticker to a person, badge, device, or other property. A sticker declined by an endpoint MUST be withdrawn without argument.

7. Error Conditions

DAVE_NOT_FOUND
BUSY
STICKERS_DEPLETED
SOCIAL_BATTERY_LOW
LINECON
UNKNOWN_DAVE_TYPE

Error conditions SHOULD be handled with patience, distance, hydration, and an ordinary human explanation where useful. If emergency food acquisition occurs during this handshake, DCP-0005 SHALL take precedence over DCP-0002.

8. Security Considerations

Social pressure is a protocol vulnerability. All optional interactions require consent. Badge inspection MUST be invited; physical contact MUST be explicitly permitted. Participants SHOULD retain an exit path that does not require a justification longer than “I’m going to get food.”

9. Durch Considerations

Durch authorities MAY classify the greeting as a rite. This classification does not alter consent requirements or the ability to end the session.
