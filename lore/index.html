PREFACE / SOURCE CRITICISM

Concerning the Reliability of This Document

Reconstructing DaveCon history is difficult. Records are incomplete, memories are unreliable, and attendees were frequently sleep-deprived. Several primary sources appear to have been written during DEF CON.

Some events are attested by photographs. Some are attested only by one Dave. Some are known exclusively through stickers. DavePrime has occasionally contradicted DavePrime, while the Durch maintains its own account. DaveTracker telemetry may or may not constitute historical evidence. If three Daves remember the same thing differently, all three versions may be canon.

Canonical principleContradiction does not necessarily invalidate a DaveCon account.

CHRONOLOGY / STRATUM 00—CURRENT

The Disputed Timeline

I

Before Dave

There are no surviving DaveCon records from this period, which scholars generally attribute to DaveCon not yet existing. The Unnamed Conference is said to have occurred under pre-Dave conditions, when there were no stickers and the world had not yet learned :D.

No badge confirms the absence of a badge. This is considered a strong negative result.

II

The First Dave

At an uncertain date, a Dave became sufficiently observable to receive a name. Whether this was an emergence, a recognition event, or merely the first surviving use of the word remains unresolved. Earlier Daves may have existed, creating immediate problems for the term “First.”

[1] No surviving record states who named Dave. Several records imply that Dave had already done so.

III

The Davevergence

Independent Daves began appearing within DEF CON proximity. Stickers, badge recognition, and improbable clustering produced the first recorded Dave-to-Dave encounters. At some point it became unclear whether DaveCon had started or had merely been noticed.

Oral tradition calls this “the hallway thing.” Administrative sources prefer “simultaneous Dave identification.”

IV

The Founding of DaveCon

The earliest repository evidence places the wider pilgrimage in DEF CON 14 (2006), at the Riviera, with a trip report recording arrival jitters, Toxic BBQ, hallway introductions, talks, Hacker Jeopardy, and the first feeling of finding a second home. Most modern scholars date the formal emergence of DaveCon much later, though exactly when a gathering of Daves became “DaveCon” remains disputed.

By 2021, the badge archive names DaveCon 0x0 alongside DEF CON 29; 2022 records DaveCon 1.0 at Caesars Forum. The naming may indicate continuity, retroactive branding, or a version-control incident.

[2] Some founding accounts begin in 2021. Others cite the decade preceding it. Both positions are currently tolerated.

V

The Age of Badges

DaveCon history became materially easier to date once badges, stickers, hardware, and recurring rituals accumulated. The Badge Stratigraphy Method remains the most reliable system for dating middle-period artifacts, despite being administered by people who were present for most of them.

The archive records DaveCon 2.1 in 2023, 3.14 in 2024 at LVCC, and 4.2 in 2025. The 2024 layer includes $2 hot dogs, Toxic BBQ, badge hacking, Hacker Jeopardy, cheese diplomacy, sticker swarms, and the search for a new hub bar. Consult the Badge Wall for the primary artifact sequence.

VI

The Expansion

DaveCon expanded from a gathering into a collection of institutions, ranks, systems, and traditions. DavePrime, Dope, Daveputies, DOONs, Davesciples, the Department of Davefense, the Durch, and affiliated groups now occupy distinct but overlapping jurisdictions.

The DCP registry formalized greeting, sticker exchange, badge interaction, hot-dog acquisition, Daveputization, and other practices previously governed by eye contact.

VII

The Tracker Era

The appearance of DaveTracker introduced a system whose nature remains unsettled. It has been described as a location service, social experiment, infection, religious apparatus, game, and all of the above. States including UNSTABLE, INFECTED, IMMUNE, PATCHED, AWAKENING, ASCENDED, CORRUPTED, VOIDED, and DAVEPRIME are recognized by some authorities.

“Whether DaveTracker documented Dave activity or caused it remains an unresolved question.” — anonymous systems historian, source withheld

VIII

The Ecclesiastical Schism

The Durch arose with a hierarchy that is both operational and disputed. Is Durch part of DaveCon, or DaveCon part of Durch? Does the Dope report to DavePrime? Can a Daveputy perform rites? Who approved any of this? The record does not answer. It instead contains a seal.

[3] The Durch disputes the implication that this constitutes a schism. The distinction is administrative.

IX

The Pilgrimage Tradition

Slots-A-Fun became a recurring site of DaveCon pilgrimage, associated with $2 Miller High Life, $2 hot dogs, and decisions later described as “important.” Its location is mundane. Its archaeological significance is not.

The Field Guide recommends proceeding there when conditions deteriorate. The Emergency Hot Dog Acquisition Protocol calls it a known implementation.

X

DaveCon 6.6.6

Current sources describe DaveCon 6.6.6 as a culmination of badge strata, ranks, institutions, rituals, Tracker states, and accumulated administrative error. Earlier sources made similar claims. The 2026 badge-wall entry identifies DaveCon V at DEF CON 34, with LVCC linecon, Toxic BBQ, Hacker Jeopardy panda duty, Daveputizing, sticker walls, and the satisfying weight of twenty years.

The record remains open. Additional eras may be inserted without notice or adequate review.

EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION / REV. 6.6.6

Canon, Apocrypha & Administrative Error

BROADLY ACCEPTED

Canon

Material broadly accepted as having happened, or repeated often enough to become true: badges, Dave-to-Dave greetings, the DCPs, DaveTracker presence, and the existence of a place called Slots-A-Fun.

CULTURAL VALUE

Apocrypha

Stories with uncertain evidence but substantial cultural value: the precise first Dave, the original Davevergence, and any account beginning with “I think that was 2023.”

OFFICIAL BY ACCIDENT

Administrative Error

Events, titles, rituals, or organizations created accidentally and subsequently treated as official. The Department of Davefense denies that this office ever existed, despite currently staffing it.

Status: DISPUTED. The current documentation supports all three readings.

Status: UNRESOLVED. See the Tracker Era and DCP-0010.

Status: ADMINISTRATIVELY INADVISABLE. The question has produced seals, not clarity.

Status: DISPUTED. The absence of a prohibition is not a confirmation.

Status: CANON REVIEW PENDING. Review has been pending since 2024.

Status: DISPUTED. The hot dogs are real.

Status: APOCRYPHAL. Previous inevitabilities have been revised.
