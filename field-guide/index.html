So You’ve Encountered a Dave

You may have arrived intentionally, accidentally, through sticker exposure, DEF CON proximity, ritual pilgrimage, DaveTracker contamination, or simple administrative error. This guide exists to help you identify Dave-related entities, understand expected conduct, and survive prolonged Dave exposure. Remain calm. Remain observant. Do not sign anything unless the pen is interesting.

FIELD NOTE: Subject may deny being Dave. This is inconclusive.

01 / taxonomy

Identification of Known Dave Forms

The following classifications are provisional, inherited, and subject to revision by someone who already had the authority.

Origin point / local apex

DavePrime

The nominal apex and origin point of local Dave activity. Treat statements from DavePrime as authoritative unless contradicted by another statement from DavePrime.

SEE ALSO: Durch clergy & bureaucracy

Senior ecclesiastical figure

Dope

Dave Pope and senior ecclesiastical figure associated with Durch. Produces proclamations, seals, and tasteful confusion. The title is not an editorial comment.

WARNING: Do not resolve Durch hierarchy in the field.

Deputized adjacency

Daveputy

A deputized agent empowered to spread and occasionally enforce the Word of Dave. Enforcement is generally limited to rules everyone already agreed were sensible.

AUTHORITY: local, ceremonial, probably.

Operational / security-adjacent

DOON

Dave Goon. Personnel assigned to operational or security-adjacent matters, including opening doors, closing loops, and watching the snacks. Mock seriousness is mandatory.

Keep tone mock-serious. Keep snacks real.

Field operations

Davesciple

An adherent, participant, recruit, or person who has failed to leave quickly enough. Studies Dave by being near Dave and taking incomplete notes.

STATUS: studying / contagious / both.

Founder / elder / archetype

DaveTangent

A shadowy founder, elder, or archetypal hacker-Dave figure associated with the lore. Officially, no such Dave exists. Unofficially, the documentation has a picture.

EDITORIAL NOTE: This paragraph is disputed by the Department of Davefense.

02 / detection

Signs of Active Dave Presence

When in doubt, consult the following field indicators. Three or more simultaneous indicators constitute a Dave-adjacent event.

:D
Emoticon recurrence

Repeated use of :D, especially in contexts where a period would have been administratively sufficient.

01
Sticker bloom

Unexplained stickers or googly eyes appearing on previously respectable objects.

VI
Terminal discussion

Discussion of vi, usually with the confidence of someone who has forgotten how to exit.

$$
Refreshment anomaly

References to $2 Miller High Life, $2 hot dogs, or both within a single administrative quarter.

△
State language

Sudden talk of ascending, stabilizing, infection, corruption, or fragments. Do not ask which fragment.

??
Title inflation

Excessive administrative titles or documentation whose acronym was invented before its full name.

FIELD NOTE: Someone insisting that a completely normal location is a pilgrimage site may be correct.

03 / standard operating procedure

Encounter Procedure

  1. Determine whether subject is Dave, Dave-adjacent, or merely confused.
  2. Establish whether stickers are being exchanged.
  3. Inspect badge hardware if invited.
  4. Determine whether DaveTracker involvement is required.
  5. Avoid unnecessary theological disputes involving Durch.
  6. If conditions deteriorate, proceed toward Slots-A-Fun.
  7. Acquire refreshments as appropriate.
  8. Continue DaveCon.
Do not panic. Panic is not currently an approved Dave protocol.In an emergency, locate a calm Dave, a charged phone, and the nearest $2 hot dog.
